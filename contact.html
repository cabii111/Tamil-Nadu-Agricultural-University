INCUBATION & PARTNERSHIPS

Connect with our incubation team

Partner with India's premier TNAU-backed agroforestry business incubator. We provide physical co-working spaces, high-tech processing labs, and seed-grant funding to help rural entrepreneurs, farmers, and forestry startups scale sustainable enterprises.

VISIT OUR CAMPUS

Our headquarters

Located within the Forest College and Research Institute campus in Mettupalayam, our facility houses state-of-the-art biomass pellet units and testing labs. Visitors must register at the main gate reception upon arrival.

Office & Laboratory Hours

Our administrative offices and technical mentoring labs are open Monday through Friday, from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, excluding public holidays.

You can find our main administrative offices and incubation facilities at the Forest College and Research Institute, Mettupalayam, Tamil Nadu 641301.

Direct Email: info@mafbif.com

Start your incubation process

Submit your business proposal or technical mentoring inquiry. Our facilitation team reviews submissions weekly to coordinate access to TNAU scientific research, EDII networks, and our specialized biomass commercialization facilities.

For immediate assistance regarding membership tiers or active training programs, you can also reach our administrative desk directly during normal campus hours.
