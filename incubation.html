The Incubation Pathway

From raw biomass to market

Our structured technology transfer process is designed to systematically de-risk and commercialize early-stage agroforestry enterprises.

STAGE 01 Intake

01. Application & Screening

Submit your business proposal online. Our joint committee of TNAU scientists and industry experts reviews applications monthly.

STAGE 02 R&D Testing

02. Technology Validation

Utilize our state-of-the-art laboratories to refine your product formulation, validate durability, and optimize extraction yields.

STAGE 03 Commercialization

03. Commercial Scale-Up

Access seed-grant funding of up to 42 Lakhs, set up trial production batches, and connect with EDII's buyer and investor networks.
