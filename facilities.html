Facilities Available

EDII-MAFBIF offers modern physical infrastructure and specialized resources to support agroforestry startups, innovators, and enterprises. From fully equipped co-working spaces and an 80-seat conference auditorium to industrial pilot yards, state-of-the-art testing labs, and practical demonstration fields, we provide the physical environment required to transition from concept to commercial reality.

Physical Facilities & Equipment

Explore the dedicated physical facilities available to incubated startups, researchers, and commercial clients at the Forest College and Research Institute, Mettupalayam.

Admin Space
01

Co-Working & Incubation Space

Modern, air-conditioned co-working stations, dedicated private cabins, high-speed fiber internet, and collaborative startup lounges. Provides entrepreneurs, student innovators, and remote teams with a professional business environment on campus.

Key Capabilities:
• 40+ Ergonomic Workstations• High-Speed Fiber Wi-Fi & LAN• Uninterrupted Power Backup• Official Mailing & Incubation Address
Book Facility Access FC&RI Campus
Admin Space
02

80-Seat Conference Auditorium

Tiered acoustic conference hall equipped with high-definition digital projection, surround sound, teleconferencing, and live-streaming infrastructure. Host webinars, investor pitch demo days, capacity-building workshops, and institutional symposiums.

Key Capabilities:
• 80 Tiered Upholstered Seats• HD Digital Projector & Smart Screen• Wireless Stage & Lapel Audio• Full Teleconferencing Capabilities
Book Facility Access FC&RI Campus
Admin Space
03

Executive Boardroom & Discussion Suites

Upscale corporate discussion room featuring a polished natural wood conference table, high-back ergonomic executive chairs, smart interactive digital display, and acoustic privacy. Ideal for mentor consultations, client negotiations, and advisory meetings.

Key Capabilities:
• 18-Seat Executive Conference Table• Interactive Touchscreen Smart Board• High-Resolution Video Conferencing• Private Negotiation Antechamber
Book Facility Access FC&RI Campus
Admin Space
04

Product Display & Exhibition Center

Dedicated institutional exhibition gallery showcasing startup prototypes, market-ready forest products, natural extracts, value-added wooden crafts, and bio-composites. Visited by visiting delegates, commercial buyers, and venture investors.

Key Capabilities:
• Illuminated Glass Display Shelving• Dedicated Startup Showcase Pedestals• Interactive Multimedia Catalogues• Direct B2B Buyer Showcase
Book Facility Access FC&RI Campus
Industrial Space
05

Bio-Processing & Industrial Pilot Facility

Heavy-duty industrial pilot manufacturing yard equipped with commercial-scale biomass briquetting presses, flash air dryers, wood seasoning kilns, timber treatment autoclaves, and CNC wood carving routers. Enables startups to trial pilot batches and scale up thermal and timber processing.

Operational Equipment & Pilot Bays:
• Biomass Briquetting Press• Flash Air Dryer & Cyclone• Wood Seasoning Kiln & Autoclave• CNC Wood Router Workshop
Book Facility Access FC&RI Campus
Lab Space
06

Plant Tissue Culture & Testing Lab

Sterile Class 10,000 biotechnology laboratory equipped with laminar airflow workstations, high-pressure autoclave sterilizers, climate-controlled culture growth rooms, and analytical spectrophotometers for micropropagation and plant health assays.

Key Capabilities:
• Sterile Laminar Airflow Cleanroom• Precision Environmental Growth Chambers• Analytical Phytochemical Profiling• Certified Disease-Free Culturing
Book Facility Access FC&RI Campus
Lab Space
07

NABARD Honey Processing & Bottling Facility

Sanitary food-grade pilot processing facility equipped with automated vacuum de-moisturization, multi-stage micro-filtration, pasteurization, and aseptic glass jar bottling. Certified to produce FSSAI-compliant commercial forest honey.

Key Capabilities:
• Food-Grade Stainless Steel (SS304)• Low-Temp Vacuum De-Moisturizing• Multi-Stage Micro-Filtration• Automated Aseptic Glass Bottler
Book Facility Access FC&RI Campus
Campus Resource
08

Demonstration & Training Grounds

Outdoor field demonstration plots, clonal multiplication polyhouses, and practical training blocks within the FC&RI campus. Allows farmers, tree growers, and entrepreneurs to experience hands-on tree management and nursery multiplication.

Key Capabilities:
• Clonal Field Trial Blocks• Automated Nursery Polyhouses• Hands-On Farmer Training Grounds• Open-Air Practical Demonstrations
Book Facility Access FC&RI Campus
