# EDII-MAFBIF | Mettupalayam Agroforestry Business Incubation Forum

> **Official Portal for EDII-MAFBIF (Tamil Nadu Agricultural University & Forest College and Research Institute Initiative)**

[![Deploy with Vercel](https://vercel.com/button)](https://vercel.com/new)

## 📌 About the Project
EDII-MAFBIF (Entrepreneurship Development and Innovation Institute - Mettupalayam Agroforestry Business Incubation Forum) is a specialized incubation ecosystem dedicated to promoting agroforestry entrepreneurship, wood science innovations, biomass value addition, and sustainable farm tech.

## 🚀 Key Pages & Features
- **Home (`/`)**: Hero, core domains, metrics, institutional trust, and news announcements.
- **About Us (`/about`)**: Vision, leadership, board of governors, and mandate.
- **Technologies (`/technologies`)**: Commercialized & pipeline agroforestry technologies.
- **Facilities (`/facilities`)**: State-of-the-art incubation laboratories, pilot plants, and equipment.
- **Incubation Overview (`/incubation`)**: Incubation models, stages, services, and application flow.
- **Members (`/members`)**: Incubated startups, enterprises, and innovators.
- **Mentors (`/mentors`)**: Academic, industrial, and technical mentoring panels.
- **Events & Training (`/events`)**: Workshops, national symposiums, and upcoming skill development schedules.
- **Success Stories (`/success-stories`)**: Case studies of successful incubatee enterprises.
- **Contact Us (`/contact`)**: Inquiries, location map, and direct outreach.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Design System
- **Markup & Styling**: HTML5, Vanilla JavaScript, Tailwind CSS (via CDN), Google Fonts (Plus Jakarta Sans & Manrope).
- **Hosting / Deployment**: Configured for zero-config automatic deployments on **Vercel** with clean URL routing.
