ABOUT MAFBIF

Empowering Innovation in Agroforestry

MAFBIF (Mettupalayam Agroforestry Business Incubation Forum) is a Section 8 not-for-profit initiative supported by EDII, Government of Tamil Nadu, and TNAU. We nurture agricultural and forestry startups, empowering entrepreneurs, farmers, and students with incubation, technology transfer, and sustainable enterprise mentorship.
