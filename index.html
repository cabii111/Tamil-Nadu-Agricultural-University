ABOUT MAFBIF

Empowering Innovation in Agroforestry

MAFBIF (Mettupalayam Agroforestry Business Incubation Forum) is a Section 8 not-for-profit organization dedicated to fostering innovation and entrepreneurship in the agroforestry sector. Supported by EDII and TNAU, MAFBIF empowers startups, farmers, students, women entrepreneurs, and tribal communities through incubation, technology transfer, skill development, mentoring, and sustainable business support, creating long-term environmental and economic impact.

FACILITIES & INFRASTRUCTURE

Modern Facilities for Research & Innovation

EDII-MAFBIF offers modern infrastructure and specialized facilities to support research, innovation, entrepreneurship, and sustainable technology development. From fully equipped co-working spaces and conference halls to advanced industrial units and laboratory facilities, we provide an ecosystem designed to foster learning, collaboration, and growth.

Co-Working Space (26 Seating Capacity)

Air-conditioned incubator workstations, dedicated high-speed fiber internet, private discussion booths, and plug-and-play setup for emerging agri-tech startup teams.

Conference & Meeting Facilities

100+ seating capacity audio-visual auditorium, interactive smart presentation screens, and executive boardroom for stakeholder summits, buyer meets, and investor pitches.

Industrial Processing Units

Pilot-scale pulverizers, briquetting machinery, industrial biomass compactors, and drying equipment for commercializing wood and non-timber forest produce.

Tissue Culture Laboratory

Controlled temperature growth chambers, laminar airflow cabinets, and autoclave systems for rapid micropropagation of elite tree clones and medicinal plants.

Honey Processing Unit

Fully mechanized moisture reduction, micro-filtration, pasteurization, and hygienic bottling setup for organic wild forest honey value-addition.

Research & Innovation Support

Direct laboratory access to analytical equipment, soil-wood durability testing protocols, and mentorship from TNAU Forest College and Research Institute scientists.
