Services & Support

EDII–MAFBIF provides comprehensive support to entrepreneurs and startups through expert mentoring, technical guidance, regulatory assistance, funding access, product development support, and strategic industry connections.

OUR SERVICES

Comprehensive Support for Every Stage

From ideation to scale, we provide end-to-end support to help you succeed.

01

INFRASTRUCTURE & RESOURCES

Infrastructure facilities include industrial space, co-working space with WiFi, conference hall and laboratory facilities.

02

CAPACITY BUILDING

Training, workshops and skill development programmes for incubatees and other beneficiaries.

03

TECHNICAL MENTORING

Experts with sound technical knowledge from institutions and industry will mentor entrepreneurs from time to time.

04

BUSINESS MENTORING

Senior corporates and established entrepreneurs will mentor startups for better business insights.

05

REGULATORY & ADVISORY SERVICES

Company formation, restructuring, legal services and related advisory support.

06

PRODUCT & SERVICE DEVELOPMENT

Validation, branding, labelling, development and launch support for products and services.

07

IPR FACILITATION

Intellectual property rights such as patent, trademark, industrial design, copyright and geographical indications.

08

NETWORKING

Networking and collaboration with all stakeholders including industry, institutions and investors.

09

FUNDING SUPPORT

Grants support through IVP voucher program and funding support through seed money and financial institutions.

WHO WE SUPPORT

Target Beneficiaries

EDII–MAFBIF supports a diverse range of individuals, innovators, startups, businesses, corporates and institutions through tailored incubation, technology, innovation and entrepreneurship support.

01

INDIVIDUALS WITH BUSINESS IDEAS

Any individuals such as students, employed or unemployed persons, women and farmers with a business idea.

02

INNOVATORS

For prototype development, IPR facilitation and grant-in-aid support.

03

START-UPS

Start-ups at any stage of development.

04

EXISTING BUSINESS ENTERPRISES

SMEs and industries looking for innovative intervention and scale-up opportunities.

05

CORPORATES

For technology transfer and CSR initiatives.

06

INSTITUTIONS

Institutions looking to bring the startup ecosystem into their campus.

07

CONSULTANCY PROJECTS

For agroforestry-related consultancy projects such as micro forestry, urban forestry, agroforestry and sustainable tourism.

STRUCTURED EXECUTION

The EDII–MAFBIF Incubation Journey

Our support services are orchestrated in stages to help early concepts transition smoothly into viable commercial enterprises.

01

Ideation & Onboarding

Initial evaluation, problem-solution alignment, and formal induction into pre-incubation or regular incubation.

02

Technical & Mentorship

Hands-on guidance with FC&RI faculty, product trials, business mentoring, and regulatory compliance.

03

IP & Funding Access

Securing intellectual property, facilitation of IVP vouchers, seed capital applications, and institutional grant linkages.

04

Launch & Scaling

Market testing, buyer-seller networking, commercial pilot operations, and investor showcases for sustained growth.
